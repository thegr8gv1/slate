# Privacy Policy for Slate

**Application:** Slate (Android Package: `com.slate.myapp`)  
**Effective Date:** October 29, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 29, 2026  

---

Slate Marketplace ("Slate", "we", "our", or "us") provides a talent discovery, casting audition, and escrow platform connecting performers, crew, casting directors, talent agencies, and production companies. We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing transparency about how your data is collected, used, and protected.

> **Core Commitment:** We **never** sell your personal data or device data to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties. Data collected is strictly used to provide casting discovery, audition recording, and escrow booking services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Camera and Audio Recordings (Self-Tape Auditions)
With your explicit in-app consent (`android.permission.CAMERA` and `android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`), Slate accesses your device camera and microphone inside the in-app Casting Room to record self-tape video auditions. Audition recordings are reviewed by you and only uploaded when you submit them to a specific casting call.

### B. Location Information (Audition Radius & Set-Beacon Alerts)
With your permission (`android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` and `android.permission.ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`), Slate accesses your approximate and/or precise location while using the app. This is used solely to:
- Calculate physical distance to shooting locations and casting calls.
- Filter and order matching opportunities within your selected travel radius.
- Broadcast emergency crew Set-Beacon alerts within your travel radius.
*Location is never tracked in the background without user initiation and is never shared with third-party advertisers.*

### C. Photos, Media, and Reels
When you upload headshots, production stills, or showreels, media files are stored securely to display on your professional profile.

### D. Profile & Professional Information
Information you provide during profile creation:
- Display name, contact email, and age/stage.
- Union membership status (SAG-AFTRA, Equity, Non-Union).
- Professional credits, specialties, representation routing, and day rate bands.

### E. Financial and Escrow Information
To facilitate escrow payments and contract settlements, banking identifiers and payout routing are processed through encrypted financial partners. Slate does not store raw payment card numbers.

### F. Device & Diagnostic Data
Technical data including device model, operating system version, and crash logs to maintain app stability and performance.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for legitimate platform operations:
1. **Discovery & Matching:** Matching talent with roles, casting calls, and audition requests.
2. **Casting Room Submissions:** Transmitting your video auditions to casting directors and producers you select.
3. **Escrow Contracts & Payouts:** Administering escrow deposits, completion verification, and platform disbursement splits.
4. **Trust & Safety Auditing:** Screening profiles to detect fraudulent engagement, bot followers, or impersonation.
5. **Notifications:** Alerting you about audition callbacks, messages, and contract status updates.

---

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We only share your information in the following circumstances:
- **Casting Directors & Productions:** When you like a project, submit an audition tape, or accept an offer, your profile and audition assets become accessible to that production team.
- **Your Talent Agency:** If you configure agency routing in Privacy Settings, booking requests and contract terms are visible to your agency.
- **Trusted Service Providers:** Cloud hosting, video encoding, and payment processors bound by strict data confidentiality agreements.
- **Legal Compliance:** When required by applicable law, court order, or governmental regulation to protect user safety and prevent fraud.

> **Discreet Mode:** For established talent, Slate offers a Discreet Mode. When enabled, your name, headshot, and sensitive credits are hidden from general browsing until an interested production meets your minimum budget threshold and signs a standard non-disclosure agreement (NDA).

---

## 4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active. You can request deletion of your account and personal data at any time:
- **In-App:** Navigate to Profile &rarr; Settings &rarr; Privacy & Routing.
- **Email Request:** Email us at [vempatigowtham@live.com](mailto:vempatigowtham@live.com) with the subject line *"Account Deletion Request"*. All profile data, video audition takes, and portfolio photos will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

---

## 5. Data Security

All communications between the app and our servers use TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption with valid SSL certificates. Sensitive stored data is encrypted at rest.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Slate is intended for individuals aged 18 and older, or minors aged 13–17 who are legally emancipated or registered under the direct supervision of a parent, legal guardian, or licensed talent agency in compliance with child performer labor regulations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
