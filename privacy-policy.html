Privacy Policy for Slate

Application: Slate (Android Package: com.slate.myapp)
Effective Date: October 29, 2026
Last Updated: October 29, 2026

Slate Marketplace ("Slate", "we", "our", or "us") provides a talent discovery, casting audition, and escrow platform connecting performers, crew, casting directors, talent agencies, and production companies. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how your data is collected, used, and shared.

Core Commitment: We never sell your personal data or device data to advertisers, brokers, or third parties. Data collected is strictly used to provide casting discovery, audition recording, and escrow booking services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Slate, we collect the following types of information:

  • Camera and Audio Recordings (Self-Tape Auditions): With your explicit in-app permission (CAMERA and RECORD_AUDIO), Slate accesses your device camera and microphone inside the Casting Room to record audition tapes. Audition takes are reviewed by you and only uploaded when you submit them to a casting call.
  • Location Information (Audition Radius & Set-Beacon Alerts): With your permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION), Slate accesses your approximate and/or precise location while using the app. This is used solely to compute distance to shooting locations, sort casting opportunities, and broadcast emergency crew Set-Beacon alerts within your specified travel radius. We do not track your location in the background without user initiation.
  • Photos, Media, and Reels: When you upload headshots, portfolios, or showreels, media files are stored on secure cloud storage to present your profile to casting professionals.
  • Profile & Professional Information: Information you provide such as your display name, email address, union membership status (SAG-AFTRA, Equity, Non-Union), professional credits, skills, representation routing, and day rate bands.
  • Payment and Escrow Information: To facilitate milestone payments and contract settlements, banking identifiers and payout routing are processed through encrypted, compliant financial partners. Slate does not store raw credit card numbers.
  • Device & Diagnostic Data: Technical data including device model, operating system version, and anonymous crash logs to ensure app reliability and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Discovery & Matching: Matching talent with roles, casting calls, and audition requests based on union status, role requirements, and location radius.
  • Casting Room Submissions: Transmitting your recorded video auditions to casting directors and producers you choose to audition for.
  • Escrow Contracts & Payouts: Administering escrow deposits, completion verification, and platform disbursement splits.
  • Trust & Safety Auditing: Screening profiles to detect fraudulent engagement, bot followers, or impersonation to maintain verified marketplace standards.
  • Notifications: Alerting you about new casting matches, callback messages, and contract status updates.

3. How We Share Your Information

We only share your information in the following circumstances:

  • With Casting Directors & Productions: When you like a project, submit an audition tape, or accept an offer, your profile and audition assets become accessible to that production team.
  • With Your Designated Talent Agency: If you configure agency routing in Privacy Settings, your booking requests and commission legs are visible to your agency.
  • With Trusted Service Providers: Cloud hosting, video encoding, and payment processors who operate under strict data protection and confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by applicable law, court order, or governmental regulation to protect the safety of users or investigate fraud.
Discreet Mode: Slate offers a Discreet Mode for established talent. When enabled, your name, headshot, and sensitive credits are hidden from general browsing until an interested production meets your minimum budget threshold and signs a standard non-disclosure agreement (NDA).

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with platform services. You have the right to request deletion of your account and personal data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Navigate to Profile → Settings → Privacy & Routing to initiate account deletion.
  • Direct Request: Email us at vempatigowtham@live.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". All associated profile data, video audition takes, and portfolio photos will be permanently deleted from our active databases within 30 days.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All communication between the app and our servers uses TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption with valid SSL certificates.

6. Children's Privacy

Slate is intended for individuals aged 18 and older, or minors aged 13–17 who are legally emancipated or registered under the direct supervision of a parent, legal guardian, or licensed talent agency in compliance with child performer labor regulations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or legal requirements. Material changes will be communicated via in-app notification or email prior to taking effect.
