# Account and Data Deletion Request - Slate

**Application:** Slate (Android Package: `com.slate.myapp`)  
**Developer Contact:** [vempatigowtham@live.com](mailto:vempatigowtham@live.com)  
**Last Updated:** October 29, 2026  

---

At Slate, we give you full control over your personal data. You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data, or to request the deletion of specific subsets of your data without deleting your account.

---

## How to Request Account or Data Deletion

### Step 1: Send an Email Request
From the email address registered with your Slate account, send an email to:
👉 **[vempatigowtham@live.com](mailto:vempatigowtham@live.com)**

### Step 2: Include the Following Information
* **Subject Line:** `Account and Data Deletion Request - Slate` or `Data Deletion Request - Slate`
* **Your Display Name:** The name shown on your Slate profile.
* **Your Registered Email:** The email address used to log into Slate.
* **Scope of Request:**
  - *Full Deletion:* Delete your entire account and all associated personal data.
  - *Partial Deletion:* Request deletion of specific items (e.g., self-tape audition takes, portfolio headshots, or location history) while retaining your account.

### Step 3: In-App Alternative
You can also initiate account closure directly inside the Slate app by navigating to:  
**Profile &rarr; Settings &rarr; Privacy & Routing**

---

## What Data Is Deleted
Upon confirmation of your request, we permanently purge:
* **Profile Information:** Display name, bio, role specialties, union status, and agency routing.
* **Audition Recordings:** All self-tape video and audio audition recordings recorded in the Casting Room.
* **Portfolio Assets:** All uploaded headshots, lookbooks, production stills, and portfolio reels.
* **Messages & Pitches:** Private messaging history and casting pitch submissions.
* **Device & Location Data:** Stored travel radius preferences and diagnostic logs.

---

## Data Retention Timeline & Legal Exceptions
* **Deletion Timeline:** All personal data requested for deletion is permanently purged from active databases within **30 days** of request verification.
* **Legal & Financial Exceptions:** Records of completed escrow contract payments and invoices are retained for up to 7 years strictly as required by applicable tax and accounting laws, after which they are permanently expunged.
