Account and Data Deletion Request

At Slate, we give you full control over your personal data. You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data, or to request the deletion of specific subsets of your data without deleting your account.

How to Request Account or Data Deletion

Follow these steps to submit a deletion request:

  1. Send an email request: From the email address registered with your Slate account, send an email to:
    Email vempatigowtham@live.com
  2. Include the following details:
    • Subject Line: "Account and Data Deletion Request - Slate" or "Data Deletion Request - Slate"
    • Your Display Name: The name shown on your Slate profile.
    • Your Registered Email: The email address used to log into Slate.
    • Scope of Request: Indicate whether you wish to delete your Entire Account & All Data, or specific data (such as audition video takes, portfolio headshots, or location history).
  3. In-App Deletion Alternative: You can also navigate directly inside the Slate app to Profile → Settings → Privacy & Routing to initiate account closure.

What Data Is Deleted

Upon confirmation of your request, we permanently purge the following data from our active systems:

  • Profile Information: Display name, contact details, bio, role specialties, union status, and agency routing.
  • Audition Media: All self-tape video and audio audition recordings recorded in the Casting Room.
  • Portfolio Assets: All uploaded headshots, lookbooks, production stills, and portfolio reels.
  • Direct Messages & Pitches: Private messaging histories and casting submissions.
  • Device & Location Data: Recorded travel radius preferences and cached diagnostic data.

Data Retention Policy & Exceptions

  • Deletion Timeline: All personal data requested for deletion is permanently removed from active databases within 30 days of request verification.
  • Legal & Financial Exceptions: Records of completed escrow contract payments and tax invoices are retained for up to 7 years in accordance with statutory accounting and anti-money laundering regulations, after which they are permanently expunged.
